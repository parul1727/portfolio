About

I'm a Senior Technical Lead with 15+ years of experience architecting scalable, high-performance web applications — from secure backend services to responsive, well-tested frontend experiences. My work spans the full stack, but my focus has always been on building systems that stay maintainable as teams and features grow.

Currently at Synchrony, I lead development on a customer-facing platform spanning authentication, payments, and account management, built with React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Express. I care about clean architecture: feature-based module structures, typed schemas, and centralized API patterns that let teams ship faster without breaking things.

Beyond writing code, I mentor developers, guide architecture decisions across cross-functional teams, and dig into the hard-to-reproduce bugs — especially around session and authentication reliability — that most people would rather avoid.
